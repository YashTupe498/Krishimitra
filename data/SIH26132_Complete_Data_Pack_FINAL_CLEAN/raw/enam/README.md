# e-NAM Data Acquisition

The official e-NAM dashboards expose the field sets represented by the `expected_schema.csv`
files in this directory tree.

## Acquisition principle
Do not fabricate e-NAM records. Save permitted exports/downloads exactly as obtained.
If the dashboard does not provide a direct CSV export, use an authorized/manual export process
and preserve a source note with acquisition date and filters.

## Relevant official pages
Historical Trade: https://www.enam.gov.in/web/dashboard/Historical
Trade Details: https://www.enam.gov.in/web/dashboard/trade-data
AGMARKNET Dashboard: https://www.enam.gov.in/web/dashboard/agmarknet
Advance Supply: https://enam.gov.in/web/advance-supply

## Target demo filters
Commodity: Onion
State: Maharashtra
District/markets: Nashik region, including Lasalgaon and Pimpalgaon Baswant where available.

## Never claim
- curated buyer demand is live e-NAM demand
- simulated logistics is an e-NAM live provider booking
- demo payment or grievance records are official transactions

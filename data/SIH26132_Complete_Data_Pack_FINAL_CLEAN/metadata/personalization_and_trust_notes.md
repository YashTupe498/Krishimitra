# Personalization, Trust and Learning Layer

These files extend the opportunity engine with farmer-specific preferences, counterfactual alternatives, outcome feedback, anomaly signals, provenance, offline behavior, sustainability scoring and crisis-mode recommendations.

## Classification
- OBSERVED/AGMARKNET-ATTRIBUTED: sourced market observations already present in the pack.
- DERIVED: calculated from existing records.
- CURATED_DEMO / CURATED_SCENARIO: illustrative records for prototype workflows.
- PROXY: explicitly non-verified stand-in values.
- REFERENCE_ESTIMATE: engineering/reference assumptions that must be replaced or validated for production.

No scenario or proxy should be presented as official live government data.
